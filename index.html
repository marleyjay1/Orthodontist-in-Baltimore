Orthodontic Treatment Options for Different Needs

Every patient has unique dental requirements. Modern orthodontics offers different treatment options designed to address a wide range of alignment and bite concerns.

Traditional Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces remain a popular option for correcting complex orthodontic problems. They use brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into better positions. Regular orthodontic appointments help monitor progress and make necessary adjustments.

Clear Braces

Clear braces provide another option for patients who want a less noticeable orthodontic appliance. They work similarly to traditional braces but use tooth-colored or clear brackets for a more discreet appearance.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable orthodontic trays designed to gradually reposition teeth. They are a convenient option for many teens and adults who prefer a less visible approach to straightening their smiles.

An orthodontic consultation can help determine whether clear aligners are appropriate for your dental condition.

Early Orthodontic Treatment for Children

Children can benefit from an orthodontic evaluation while their teeth and jaws are still developing. Early assessments help orthodontists identify potential alignment and bite problems before they become more complicated.

An orthodontist may recommend early treatment when there are concerns involving jaw development, crowded teeth, or bite irregularities.

Parents searching for a reliable orthodontist in Baltimore should consider scheduling an evaluation to understand their child's orthodontic needs. Early guidance can help determine the right time for treatment.

Straightening your teeth offers benefits beyond appearance. Properly aligned teeth can make everyday dental care easier and help improve how your upper and lower teeth fit together.
